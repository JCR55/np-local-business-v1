About Station Garage, Usk

Welcome to Station garage Usk, an independent Garage providing a range of services to customers throughout the local community and Monmouthshire since 1957. Our range of services include Car and light commercial vehicle servicing, maintenance and repairs. We carry out Manufacturers recommended Service Schedules for all Makes of vehicle. We are a Vosa approved and Accredited Vehicle Testing Station which enables us to carry out MOT testing for both petrol and diesel vehicles (class iv).
