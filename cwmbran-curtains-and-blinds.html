About Cwmbran Curtains & Blinds

Cwmbran Curtains and Blinds we are an established local company based on Larch Grove, Malpas with over 50 years of combined knowledge and experience in this complex and growing market we offer the highest standards in both our made to measure department and ready made curtains and Blinds where ever your budget takes you.

All types of blinds from vertical to wood Venetian roller or Roman.

From the initial selection of fabric to the final fitting stage we will meet with the highest of standards and make sure that you the customer will be pleased with your final choice.

You can visit us or Showroom or arrange an appointment we can bring of our extensive range of fabrics to your home for selection where our expert advice is always on hand.
