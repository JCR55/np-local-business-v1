About Maria's Flowers

Maria's Flowers is a family-run florist based in Cwmbran town centre, supplying fresh and artificial flowers for birthdays, anniversaries, funerals, weddings and other special occasions.

The shop offers daily deliveries of fresh flowers and same-day delivery where orders are placed before 12 o'clock. Customers can order online through the Maria's Flowers Wales website or visit the stall in Cwmbran Shopping Centre for friendly advice and personal service.

From seasonal flowers and online orders to wedding flowers and funeral tributes, Maria's Flowers helps local customers choose thoughtful arrangements for life's important moments.
