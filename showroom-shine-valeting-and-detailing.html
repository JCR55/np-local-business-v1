About Showroom Shine Valeting & Detailing

A well-established business, Showroom Shine started out in 2016 and has built a strong reputation over the last 10 years.

Having a passion for vehicles going back far earlier, we have extensive experience in vehicle detailing, paint correction and automotive artwork.

We pride ourselves on our honesty, professionalism and willingness to help, regardless of the vehicle type or owner's budget.

Whilst our personal passion is classic Ford vehicles, we appreciate that every car has a proud owner who only wants the best for their pride and joy.

We have solid knowledge of how paintwork or vehicle signage can make something look special, with work carried out in our purpose-built workshop.

With honest knowledge and advice, we can guide you in the right direction for whatever you require us to carry out on any mode of transport you may have.
