About Groves Gas

Groves Gas is a family-centred business founded in 2006 with the aim of being the leading independent gas, plumbing and heating business in Wales. The team prides itself on putting customers first without compromising on safety or quality.

Based in Cwmbran, Groves Gas supports customers with oil, LPG, wood burners, plumbing and heating services, alongside showroom products including stoves, fires, outdoor fire pits, gas stoves, electric stoves and stove accessories.

The business has built its reputation on professional service, reliable workmanship and practical support for homes and businesses across South East Wales.
