About Mosswood Industrial Supplies

Mosswood Industrial Supplies is based on Court Road Industrial Estate in Cwmbran and supplies a broad range of tools, equipment and workshop essentials for trade, industry, home and garden customers.

Since being established in 1993, Mosswood has focused on making practical work easier by offering modern tools and supplies across areas including hand tools, power tools, compressors, pneumatic tools, welding and cutting equipment.

Customers can visit the Cwmbran shop for everyday workshop products, accessories, fixings, woodworking supplies, gardening equipment, auto and engineering products and safety wear, with friendly local service and practical advice.
