Privacy Policy

How NP Local Business collects, uses and protects your personal data.

Last updated: 8 September 2026

NP Local Business ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, how it is used, and your rights under the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

Who we are

NP Local Business is a local business directory promoting trusted independent businesses across the NP postcode area. For any privacy queries, contact us at nplocalbusiness@outlook.com.

What data we collect

  • Contact and join form submissions. When you use our contact or "Join" forms, we collect the details you provide, which may include your name, business name, email address, telephone number, category and any message or referral details. These submissions are processed and delivered to us via our form provider, Web3Forms.
  • Member business listing details. If your business is listed with us, we hold the details you or we add to your listing, such as business name, description, contact details, address, services, images and social or website links.
  • Basic server logs. Our hosting and content delivery provider, Cloudflare, automatically records basic technical logs for every visit (such as IP address, browser type and requested pages) for security, performance and abuse-prevention purposes.

We do not currently use analytics or marketing cookies. See our Cookie Policy for full details on the only cookie-like storage this site uses.

Why we use your data and our lawful basis

  • To respond to enquiries submitted through our contact and join forms (legitimate interest / consent).
  • To create, maintain and display a business's listing on the directory, where that business has agreed to be listed (contract / legitimate interest).
  • To keep the site secure and operating reliably, using Cloudflare's basic server logs (legitimate interest).

Who we share your data with

We use a small number of trusted third-party services to run this site:

  • Cloudflare — hosting, content delivery and security for the website, including basic server logs.
  • Web3Forms — processes contact and join form submissions and delivers them to our inbox.
  • Decap CMS / DecapBridge — the content management system we use to create and update business listing content on the site.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not share it with any third party for their own marketing purposes.

How long we keep your data

Enquiry and join form submissions are kept only as long as needed to respond to you and, where relevant, set up or maintain a business listing. Business listing details are kept for as long as the listing remains active, plus a reasonable period afterwards for our records. Server logs are retained by Cloudflare only for a short period for security purposes.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Ask us what personal data we hold about you and request a copy of it.
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Ask us to delete your data, where there is no legal reason for us to keep it.
  • Object to, or ask us to restrict, certain uses of your data.
  • Ask for your data to be provided to you in a portable format.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at nplocalbusiness@outlook.com. If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data, you also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

Children

This website and its forms are intended for business owners and adult members of the public, not children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time, for example if the data we collect or the third-party services we use change. Any changes will be posted on this page.

Contact us

If you have any questions about this privacy policy or how we handle your data, please email nplocalbusiness@outlook.com.
