Cookie Policy

What cookies and similar storage this site uses, and why.

Last updated: 8 September 2026

This policy explains the cookies and similar storage technologies used on the NP Local Business website. It should be read alongside our Privacy Policy.

What we use

We currently use a single, essential item of browser storage to remember the choice you make on our cookie banner. We do not use any analytics, advertising or marketing cookies.

Name Type Duration Purpose
np_cookie_consent Essential — browser localStorage (not a tracking cookie) 12 months Remembers whether you accepted or declined our cookie banner, so it isn't shown again on every visit.

No analytics or marketing cookies — yet

At present, NP Local Business does not use any analytics, advertising or marketing cookies or tracking technologies. If that changes in the future, we will update this page to list the new cookies, their purpose, and how to opt out, before they are set.

Managing storage in your browser

You can clear or block localStorage for this site at any time through your browser's privacy or site-data settings. Doing so will simply cause the cookie banner to appear again on your next visit.
