About Cwmbran Tuning & Service Centre

Cwmbran Tuning & Service Centre is a friendly, professional garage serving motorists across Cwmbran, Pontypool, Newport and the surrounding areas.

With decades of combined experience, the qualified team provides dependable MOT testing, vehicle servicing, diagnostics and mechanical repairs for cars, vans, motorcycles and scooters.

Customers can expect honest advice, quality parts, reliable workmanship and competitive prices from an experienced local team.
