About Andrews Jewellers

Andrews Jewellers Pontypool is a family run business which has providing Jewellery, watch, clock, sales and repair from their shop on George Street for the past 35 years.

The family pride themselves on the quality of workmanship, skills that have been handed down through the family. All repairs are under taken on the premises wherever possible.
